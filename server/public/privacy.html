Privacy Policy — ParkCast Player

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This policy covers the ParkCast Player application (package com.parkcast.player), published on Google Play by UnlimitedFun LLC. ParkCast Player is a digital-signage display application: it turns a television into a screen that shows media managed by the venue that operates it.

Data we collect

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information from anyone. It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, profiling cookies, or third-party SDKs. It does not access contacts, location, microphone, camera, or personal files on the device. It requires no account and no sign-in.

What the app transmits, and why

The app connects only to the signage server operated by the venue that owns the screen. It sends that server two things: a randomly generated screen identifier (so the venue's dashboard can tell its screens apart) and playback status (which media item the screen is currently showing). Both exist solely so the venue can manage its own screens. Neither identifies any person, and no information about viewers is collected or transmitted.

Who receives data

Only the signage server the screen is connected to. By default that is the hosted ParkCast service, which UnlimitedFun LLC operates solely to run the venue's screens; a venue may instead point the app at its own self-hosted server. Nothing is sent to advertisers, analytics providers, or any other third party, and UnlimitedFun LLC does not use the data for anything beyond operating the service.

Security

Communication with the venue's server uses HTTPS when the server is reachable over the public internet. The venue's management dashboard is password-protected, and new screens must be approved there before they receive content.

Retention and deletion

The screen identifier is kept on the device and on the venue's server only while the screen is paired. Uninstalling the app deletes everything the app stored on the device. A venue operator can delete a screen (and its identifier and playback history) from the dashboard at any time. To request deletion of anything else, contact us at the address below — requests are honored within 30 days.

Children

The app is a display tool intended for business operators. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.
