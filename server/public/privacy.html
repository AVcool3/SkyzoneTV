Privacy Policy — Skyzone TV Player

Last updated: September 2026

Skyzone TV Player is a digital-signage display application. It turns a television into a screen that shows media managed by the venue that operates it.

Data the app collects

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, cookies for profiling, or third-party SDKs. It does not access contacts, location, microphone, camera, or files on the device.

What the app does transmit

The app connects only to the signage server address entered during setup (operated by the venue). It sends that server a randomly generated screen identifier and playback status (which item is currently showing) so the venue's dashboard can manage its screens. No information about viewers is collected or transmitted.

Permissions

Internet — to reach the venue's signage server. Run at startup / display over other apps — so the player can resume automatically after the device restarts. These permissions are used for no other purpose.

Children

The app is a venue display tool intended for business operators. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
