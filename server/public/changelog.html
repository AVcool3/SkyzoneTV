PARKCAST

Changelog

What's new in ParkCast, newest first.

October 2026

  • New website and sign-in experience — clearer setup and pricing information, a cleaner login with show-password and proper labels.
  • Screens keep playing without internet. Each TV now stores its content on-device: media downloads once per screen, loops play from local storage, and playback survives outages — even a reboot while the connection is down.
  • Hands-free updates. Screens quietly pick up player improvements on their own; nobody touches a TV after a release.
  • Fire TV playback fix — removed a play icon some Fire TV models drew over videos.

September 2026

  • Multi-venue platform. Self-serve signup; every venue gets its own isolated workspace — screens, media, playlists, parties, and team.
  • Team accounts. Owners run setup and access; staff run the day. Password changes and resets built in.
  • Support access, on your terms. ParkCast support can only see a venue while its owner grants time-boxed access, revocable at any moment.
  • Delete your data yourself. Account and whole-venue deletion from the dashboard, effective immediately. Public policy pages added.
  • PowerPoint import. Drop a .pptx into Media and each slide becomes a screen-ready image.
  • Per-venue timezones for imported party times and day schedules.

Summer 2026

  • Birthday takeovers. Bookings become automatic, themed takeovers — the right name on the right room's screen at party time, then back to the regular loop.
  • Studio designer, playlists, themes, CSV booking import, and the Android TV player app.
