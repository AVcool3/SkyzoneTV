Delete Your Account and Data — ParkCast

Applies to the ParkCast Player app (package com.parkcast.player) and the ParkCast dashboard, operated by UnlimitedFun LLC.

If you watched a screen in a venue

Nothing to delete: the app collects no data from viewers. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics. See the privacy policy.

If a ParkCast app is installed on your TV

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the device. The venue that paired the screen can also delete it (and its identifier and playback status) from their dashboard at any time — open the screen's menu and choose Delete.

If you have a dashboard account

You can delete your account yourself, without contacting anyone:

  1. Sign in to your ParkCast dashboard.
  2. Press Delete my account at the bottom of the left sidebar.
  3. Confirm with your password.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. If yours is the venue's last account, the venue is deleted with it — every uploaded file, screen, playlist, and party is erased from the server. If other members remain, only your account and its sessions are removed (a venue's only owner must first make another member an owner). A venue owner can also erase the entire venue at once with Delete this venue on the Team page.

What is deleted

Your account (email, name, password hash) and all its sign-in sessions; and, when the venue is deleted, all of its uploaded media files, text slides, layout designs, playlists, folders, party events, screen records, and member accounts. There is no retained copy — removal from the server is immediate.
