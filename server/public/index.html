Setup

On the TVs you already own — here's exactly what that means

1
Plug a streaming stick into the TV Any Fire TV or Google TV stick works — the ones venues already have behind half their TVs.
2
Open ParkCast Player on it The screen shows a 6-digit code. That's the whole setup on the TV side.
3
Type the code in your dashboard Press “+ Add screen”, name it after its room, choose what plays. About two minutes per TV.
Works with
  • Amazon Fire TV Stick (any recent model)
  • Chromecast / Google TV and Android TV devices
  • Smart TVs with a browser, mini-PCs, and tablets
  • Your own on-premise server, if you prefer LAN-only
No special hardware to buy from us — a ~$25 stick turns any TV in the building into a managed screen.
