Guides

The short version of everything a venue needs. Stuck anywhere? Email us — a real person answers.

Add a screen

  1. Plug a Fire TV / Google TV stick into the TV (or open a browser on any smart display) and open ParkCast Player.
  2. The TV shows a 6-digit code.
  3. In your dashboard, press + Add screen, type the code, and name the screen after its room. About two minutes per TV.

Upload media — including PowerPoint

Media → Upload media. Videos (MP4/WebM/MOV), photos, and .pptx files all work — each PowerPoint slide becomes a screen-ready image. Use Replace file on any item to swap the file while keeping it in every playlist.

Playlists

Build a playlist from your media, set per-item durations and transitions, then assign it to one screen, several, or every screen at once from the Screens page.

Birthday parties

Parties → + Add party (or import bookings). Give it a name, age, room, time, and theme. At party time the room's screen switches to a personalized takeover on its own, then returns to its regular loop. Start now runs any party early.

Your team

Team → + Add member. Owners manage setup, access, and deletion; staff run the day-to-day. Any owner can reset a member's password.

If the internet drops

Nothing happens on the TVs — screens store their content on-device and keep playing, even through a reboot. The dashboard reconnects automatically when your connection returns.

Start and end of day

Start day wakes every screen; End day blanks them. Screens stay paired either way.
