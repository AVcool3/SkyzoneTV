# Incident list

Every production problem gets a dated entry — added by the hourly reliability
agent when a venue health check or CI fails, or by hand when a human notices
something the checks missed (a frozen screen, a takeover that fired wrong, a
staff complaint). This list, not intuition, sets the post-launch engineering
order (see docs/STRATEGY.md).

Format per entry:

    ## YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM — <venue or CI> — <one-line summary>
    - Impact: who/what was affected, for how long
    - Cause: what actually happened (fill in after diagnosis)
    - Fix: what resolved it, and what prevents the repeat
